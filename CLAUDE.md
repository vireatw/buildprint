# 築跡 Buildprint — project guide for Claude Code

Live site: https://buildprint.tw · Repo: `vireatw/buildprint` · Owner: Danny (GitHub `vireatw`)

A construction daily-report app (工地日報) for Taiwanese job sites. Crews and machines are entered per day (整日／上午／下午／晚上), with work items per crew and per machine, photos, notes, 打卡, an annotated site plan, and an isometric 「工地動畫」 that replays each day from the reports. It started as a Claude artifact and moved here in Nov 2026.

## How to talk to Danny
- Reply in **Traditional Chinese (Taiwan)** unless he writes in English. Keep answers short, about one phone screen.
- He is not a programmer and often works from an **iPhone or iPad**. Give tap-by-tap steps for anything outside the code (Firebase console, GitHub settings, Porkbun).
- After every push, tell him what changed and to open `https://buildprint.tw/?v=<new number>` (GitHub Pages caches for about 10 minutes).
- He cares a lot about **alignment and visual polish**. Check spacing, edges and baselines before calling a UI change done.

## Standing rules
- **日報 and 工地動畫 must stay in sync, both ways.** Anything entered in 填日報 (crews, machines, vendors, counts, shifts, each crew's and machine's 工作事項 and zone) must show in the animation; anything added, moved or deleted in the animation's 編輯 mode must be written back to that day's report. Check both directions before pushing.
- Machines count as a 工種 and each machine as one person in the 工種 summary.
- Don't put real people's or companies' data in examples or demo data beyond what is already there.

## How the data layer works
`index.html` only talks to `window.claude.use('db' | 'user' | 'assets' | 'downloads')`. `window.BP_WEB` (stub in `<head>`, filled by backend.js) is non-null only on the website; `init()` awaits `BP_WEB.ready`, which resolves after sign-in and membership.
- **db**: Firestore-like wrapper: `doc(path).get/set/update/delete/onSnapshot`, `collection(path).orderBy/limit/where/get/onSnapshot`. Snapshots have `exists` (boolean), `data()`, `docs`, `metadata.fromCache`. Listeners use `includeMetadataChanges` and offline persistence.
- **Paths**: `site/info` (main site), `sites/{id}` (other sites), `reports/{YYYY-MM-DD}` and `sites/{id}/reports/{date}`, `punch/{uid}`, `visits/{uid}`, `profiles/{uid}` (`{name}`), `members/{uid}` (`{role:'edit'|'view', code, name, email, at}`), `admins/{uid}`, `joinCodes/{code}` (`{role}`), `settings/codes` (`{edit, view}`), `blobs/{id}` + `blobs/{id}/parts/{000…}`.
- **Weather**: `SITE.loc={name,lat,lon}` (設定 → 基本資料 → 工地地點: 縣市 + 鄉鎮市區 from `TW_TOWNS`, or GPS; also `city`, `town`, `gps`) turns on automatic weather from Open-Meteo (free tier, no key, non-commercial; `wxLoad` in index.html). Past days are saved in `SITE.wxlog[date]={am,pm,mm,hi,lo}` by anyone who can write; today/tomorrow come from the forecast. A 日報's own weather always wins; days without a report use the log (工地動畫, the 下雨天數 card in 歷史紀錄). New reports are pre-filled from it.
- **Building / 圖說** (設定 → 樓層、圖說): `SITE.floors=[{name,base,top,stage?}]` (heights in cm from the elevation drawing) and `SITE.bLen` (building's long side in m) drive the floor-by-floor building in 工地動畫: footprint = the work zones' own outline, notches kept (`zonesOutline` → `footprintGeo`; not their convex hull), replaced by the 完成圖 outline when the layout JSON has `outline` (`outlineGeo` fits it onto the zones and sets metres per grid unit from the drawing, so `SITE.bLen` is then unused; it turns only with the site's own lines — `domAngle` of the beams, else of the zone edges — and keeps every wall at least 0.08 grid units inside the fence), each floor follows the 工程階段 of the same name (`floorStage`/`stageOn`; 進行中 = red steel frame, 完成 = grey frame + deck, clad once a stage named 外牆 is done). Stages keep `since`/`doneOn` dates so past days show the building as it was. Crews in the footprint stand on the active floor. Drawn in `drawBuilding` (2D fallback) and `buildBldg` in sim3d.js (3D). 剖開看 (`SIM.cut`, the layers button on 工地): the chosen floor shows its walls cut at ~1.2 m and room names, from `SITE.layout={id,floors}` — a JSON blob `{floors:{<floor name>:{s:[x1,y1,x2,y2,…] cm, t:[[name,x,y],…]}}}` converted from the 建照圖 DXF (y up; since Nov 2026 the cut view follows the signed 建照 drawings, 第一次變更設計, not the 完成圖; a line counts as a wall only where the signed PDF A2.01–A2.06 plots it as a cut wall or column, line weight ≥0.72 pt), fitted onto the footprint by `layoutFit` (mirror, same area, best-overlap rotation). A floor may also carry `v:[[x1,y1,…],…]`, holes in its slab. Only real walls go in `s`: when converting, an X drawn in a box (挑空, 管道間, 梯井) becomes a `v` hole instead of two walls, and door swings, wheelchair turning circles and other arcs of 50–140 cm radius (rounded stair outlines too) are left out. 外觀: the same layout JSON may carry `facade={xr,yr,open1F,roof:{ridgeX},sides:{yn,yp,xp,xn:[[from,to,z0,z1,type],…]}}` (openings measured off the 建照立面圖 A3.01–A3.03, cm; type 0 glass, 1 louver, 2 open bay such as the parking entrance), `extras:{side:[[from,to,z0,z1,off0,off1,colour],…]}` (piers, canopies, parapets standing off the longest wall of that side) `pv:[[x0,y0,x1,y1],…]` (solar panels from A7.03, cm, laid on the roof slope) and `rods:[[x,y,h],…]` (避雷針 masts from A7.04, h = height above the roof, cm); `facadeFit` gives each footprint wall the elevation it faces and sim3d.js draws the windows, the ground floor and the cross-ridge roof. `SITE.drawings=[{id,name,set,aspect,pdfId?}]` are blobs grouped by set (建照圖／完成圖／立面圖／其他).
- **Foundation and fence** (工地動畫): `SITE.beams` are drawn as traced in the plan editor, but `fenceClamp` pulls any beam that runs along the fence (within its form width or column head plus a small gap) inside, and the beams meeting it follow along their own lines, so 地樑 and column heads never stand on or past the 圍籬. Night light comes from point lights at the site's corners while anyone works after dark (no light towers are drawn).
- **Firestore cannot store nested arrays.** Use arrays of objects (e.g. punch `log: [{k:'in', at}]`).
- **Photos and drawings** are stored in Firestore, base64 in 700 KB parts (free Spark plan, no Firebase Storage). `imgSrc({id})` → `BP_WEB.blobSrc(id)`, which returns a placeholder (`…#bp-<id>`) and swaps the real image into every matching `<img>` once loaded.
- **Roles**: admin (in `admins/`), 可編輯 (`edit`), 只能看 (`view`). `USER.can('data.write')` is true for admin and edit. Everyone, including 只能看, may 打卡 for themselves.
- **Joining**: a new user registers with an invite code; the rules let them create only their own `members/{uid}` with that code's role.

## Foundation ↔ floor plans alignment (Nov 2026)
- Every floor of the 平面配置 shares one grid (A=0, axis 1=714 cm; checked against the bubbles of A2.01–A2.06 and S1.01–S1.06).
- `plan.at` in the 平面配置 = two grid points with their place on the plan drawing (0–1). `planFit` puts the building exactly there (`fit.exact`); it is ignored when the drawing's aspect differs or the drawn 地樑 no longer follow `beams`, and the old zone fit takes over.
- `beams` = 地樑 centre lines of the structural drawing (cm). With an exact fit, `beamSnap` moves hand-drawn beams onto them (reach 150 cm) before `fenceClamp`. SITE.beams itself is never rewritten.
- `margin` (cm) = how far outside the outline a line may lie and still belong to a floor (default 60). Site lines (boundary wall, ditches) are removed when the file is built.
- The sim is rebuilt once when the 平面配置 finishes loading (simMount key), so the beams can settle.

## 合作 (same 工作事項 → work together, Nov 2026)
- Tasks of the day whose text is identical (spaces ignored) and that belong to two or more trades/machines form a team (`teams` in simBuild).
- 怪手 + 卡車類 (卡車、土車、砂石車…) with a soil-haul task: `haul` — digger by a soil pile, truck beside it off the soil, digger loads it (`v.loadTo`, `v.earth`, truck `eT` drives the digger's swing in sim3d).
- Other machines of a team draw up 3 units from the one that stays put (truck/pump/mixer); a machine moved by hand that day stays where it was put.
- Crews of a team: `w.mate` (stand round the machine, further out round a digger/crane) or, with no machine, `w.spot` (one spot in the task's zone). Special roles (flagger, pour, carry, pipe, wash, clean, engineers) are left alone.
- Soil piles are solid for parking and truck routes (`soilP` in `dProp`).
- Drag (編輯): the haul pair (`machHaul`: digger with `loadTo` + `earth` truck) is moved one machine at a time (Danny's choice); `haulDrop` brings the other beside it only when the bucket could no longer reach the bed (1.9–3.2 units). Every other machine is dragged on its own too (`machGroup(v)` returns just `v`; Danny: dragging one must never move the others); once the team's anchor was placed by hand that day, its teammates keep their own places. Dropping a machine or a worker on another (or a machine on a soil pile) asks 一起合作？ and writes the shared 工作事項 to the 日報 (`teamAsk`/`teamJoin`/`soilJoin`). The haul pair's place is saved as `SITE.mpos['@haul']` ({from,x,y,a truck; gx,gy,ga digger}) and simBuild uses it before searching; a haul digger ignores its own mpos entry.
- 山貓 placed by hand (any `SITE.mpos` entry, or dropped on a soil pile → entry with `soil:1`) gets `v.stay`: `skidStep` loads at that spot (facing `v.fa`) and shuttles from there instead of running to the nearest 怪手. `soilJoin`/`teamJoin` clear `SIM.key` so the sim is rebuilt (the simMount key does not include `SITE.mpos`).
- **Machines never touch** (Danny, Nov 2026). `vBody(v)` = a machine's body as circles (a digging 怪手 includes its creep and arm), `vClash`/`vPen` test overlap, `vPair` exempts the pairs that meet on purpose (預拌車 at its pump, 怪手 loading its 土車). simBuild moves any 山貓／small machine／怪手／吊車 standing in another (or on the trucks' way in) to the nearest clear spot (`vFreeSpot`); trucks, pumps and mixers keep their places. In simUpdate a move or turn into another machine is undone (the machine waits); the 山貓 treats machines ahead like people and picks another way, and only loads/dumps where it is clear. Dropping a machine on another in 編輯 nudges it clear (`machSave`).
- **怪手 digging** (sim3d `animVehicle`, Nov 2026): one bucket cycle (8.5 s, or the truck's 9 s `eT` when loading): reach out with the bucket open → teeth into the ground (pit floor or the face of the soil pile in front, via `soilTop`) → crowd the stick in so the teeth drag through the soil while the bucket curls shut → lift → swing to the dump side (`v.dumpSide`, chosen in simBuild where there is room; the truck's bed when loading) → open the bucket, clods (`u.clods`) fall → swing back. Fixed-length two-link arm (boom 1.38, stick 1.42). The tracks no longer creep back and forth while digging.
- **怪手 sizes** (Nov 2026): 微型／小型／中型／大型 = `DIG_SC` {xs:0.6, s:0.82, m:1.1, l:1.38} (the model itself is 1; Danny asked for every size a little bigger). Kept on the 日報 as `r.msize={<machine name>:'m,s'}`, one code per unit (`digSizeOf`; a name such as 小怪手 sets the default). Chosen in 填日報 (a row per unit under the 怪手 card, `digSzHTML`) and in the animation's 編輯 bar (微 小 中 大, `islDigSize`), both write the same field. simBuild sets `v.sc`/`v.dsz`; sim3d scales the whole machine and works the arm in the machine's own units; reach, body circles (`vBody`), stand-off from the truck (`digOff`) and from soil piles all scale with it.
- **進料 (material deliveries)** (Nov 2026): a 工作事項 that `isDeliv` (進料、卸料、到貨、吊料… or 進場 with a known material) makes the truck a delivery (`v.deliver`, when nothing is being hauled away that day — 載出 wins): it arrives loaded (`cargo` 3), the 吊卡's crane runs the lift backwards (`v.lift.out`) a bundle at a time onto the stack beside it (`st.drop`, `mats[]` per layer), leaves empty and comes back with the next delivery in turn (`v.dl`, `v.trip`). `matOf(text)` picks the look — ply, rebar, steel, scaf, pipe, block, bag, glass, box — drawn by `matLayer`/`MATS` in sim3d (truck bed, hook bundle, stack). Crews fetch from the stack (`w.role='carry'` + `w.rev`): those whose own 工作事項 is the delivery, plus a third of the crew whose material it is (`MAT_KIND`); with nobody to fetch, the stack is used up slowly. A truck is shown even if none is on the 日報.
- **工作事項的時段** (Nov 2026): a task may carry `half` ('am'|'pm'|'ng'; 'all' or none = whole day), set by the pill on its row in 填日報 (cycles 全天→上午→下午→晚上) or read from its words (`taskHalf`: 上午／下午／晚上 in the text). simBuild drops the tasks of another half when the screen shows 上午／下午／晚上 (today: the half picked, else the time of day), so e.g. 上午模板進料 and 下午鋼筋進料 each run in their own half; 全天 view shows all.
- **柱位與梁柱尺寸** (plan editor, Nov 2026): `SITE.beams[i]` may carry `w`,`d` (寬×深, cm; none = 預設) and `SITE.cols=[{x,y,w,d}]` (plan coords 0–1, cm) are columns placed with the 柱 tool (tap to place, drag to move, 地樑交點自動放柱, size selects in the hint bar; `PE.g.cols`, `PE.csel`). In simBuild sizes are drawn against the model's own beam (45×60 → `wk`,`hk` on each seg) and column (60×60 → `cw`,`cd`). With any column placed, only those stand (snapped to the nearest beam joint within 0.6) and beams run on into each other at joints without one; with none placed, every joint gets the default column head as before. A dug zone is as deep as its tallest beam needs (`z.bh`).
